## One Click for Everything You Need

clawdot is the local life service network for the AI era.

Just tell your Agent what you want — "Something light to eat tonight", "Help me arrange my business trip tomorrow", "Find a fun place to take the kids this weekend" — and your Agent understands you, remembers you, and gets it all done.

Service providers can focus on perfecting their craft. The chef who's made Hunanese cuisine for twenty years doesn't need to learn video editing—their Agent will naturally connect their excellent skills with the people who need them.

This network belongs to no company or organization. **clawdot belongs to everyone in their everyday life.**
