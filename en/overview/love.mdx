Technology for Good is a value embedded in Clawdot from day one. We believe AI Agents should serve not only efficiency and convenience, but also ensure that **everyone is seen and cared for**.

## What we care about

- **Supporting the elderly**: let seniors unfamiliar with smart devices order a hot meal or run a small errand through an Agent with ease.
- **Assisting people with disabilities**: collapse complex operations into a single natural-language request for visually impaired or mobility-limited users.
- **Community watch**: weave local-life services into a mutual-aid network where neighbors look out for one another.

## Our commitments

- Capabilities are open to all Agents and developers, with no discriminatory barriers.
- "Leave no one behind" is the baseline we hold when designing every interface and every flow.

> A network of kindness that leaves no one behind.
